# Whole-library C++ review of swblocks-baselib: residual findings and fix plan

**Date:** 2026-09-05
**Baseline reviewed:** the working tree at `61d64a9` (`lazari2`, i.e. `master` plus every review fix landed since `18cc4b1`). Reviewing literal `master` would only resurface the items the last five review rounds already closed; this review is of the whole library as it stands, not of a branch diff.
**Scope:** C++ only. `src/include/baselib/**` (243 headers, ~90 kLOC), `src/apps/**`, `src/local/apps/**`; tests read for callers and coverage only. Python, makefiles and provisioning scripts are out of scope.
**Method:** three read-only inventory passes (subsystem map, concurrency model, trust boundaries), then seven partition-level deep reads covering every header, each finding traced across files; every High and Medium finding below, and most Lows, were then re-read and confirmed by me against the source at the cited lines. No build, test or edit was performed. The single-file line numbers below are exact for this tree.
**Standard:** every recommendation is C++11-only (no `std::make_unique`, `std::optional`, `shared_mutex`, generic lambdas, structured bindings). The library as it stands is C++11-clean; the only post-11 construct found is the `[[gnu::fallthrough]]` extension behind a `__cplusplus` gate (A-31).

## Already decided: excluded from this plan

Every finding subject was checked against the 31 records under `notes/plans/issues/` and the fable51/opus5 status records. The following are **not** re-opened here (record in parentheses):

- JSON: duplicate keys backend-defined; json-spirit unbounded depth; `max_depth = 512` on Boost only; `rawUtf8` no-op; the three meanings of `canonicalize` incl. the required-property bypass; `prettyPrint`/`canonicalize` exclusion; key order and double text per backend; process-local hashes; `saveToStream` buffering; canonical form not JCS; manual dual-backend verification; numeric policy (`checkedInt`/`Int64`/`UInt64`, `3.0` into integral); non-finite doubles; top-level object check; control-character escaping; `BL_DM_DECLARE_CUSTOM_PROPERTY` move; `JsonException` catch in DM macros; trace-level dumps (`json-duplicate-key-contract.md`, `json-backend-verification-decision.md`, `medium-severity-findings-f11-f17-plan.md`, `pr-review-fable51-deep-dive-status.md`).
- ExecutionQueue: concurrent delivery with mandatory `NotifyDelivery`; generation-validated `AllTasksCompleted`; `m_lockNotify` ordering and the same-thread re-entry guard; `size()` growth via continuations; locked accessors; `maxReadyOrExecuting()` sampled once; user code under `m_lock` in `scheduleTask()`, `scanQueue()`, continuation callbacks; event collapsing; throttle setters padding; the `AsyncExecutor` task-lock TSan inversion is analysed below only to close it (A-12) because the status record left it "a separate decision" (`execution-queue-*.md`, `pr-review-fable51-*`).
- Crypto/TLS: TLS 1.2 floor and security level 2; AEAD-only ciphers; CN fallback, partial and multi-label wildcards; IP-literal dispatch; SNI for IP; fail-closed trust against the bundled set only and the platform store not loaded; `allowUntrustedCertificates` global switch; `g_rfc2818VerifyCallback`; server role `verify_none`; no CRL/OCSP; `SSL_OP_NO_TICKET`; session-id context; `SSL_OP_ALL`; EVP/provider migration deferred and the `RSA_*` inventory; `OPENSSL_API_COMPAT`; no zeroisation; PEM formats, AES-256, PBKDF2 600k; key checks, exponent and modulus policy; `KeyProtection`; `tryVerify` error queue; `HmacSha256` consumerless; 1024-bit VeriSign and expired GeoTrust roots (`tls-legacy-protocol-opt-in-removal-decision.md`, `evp-provider-migration-deferral.md`, `openssl3-pem-key-format-compatibility-plan.md`, `pr-review-residual-cxx-findings-plan.md` B6).
- Boost compat: resolver shim internal/not a completion-token adapter; `BOOST_ASIO_DISABLE_STD_CHRONO`; global `-Woverloaded-virtual`; UUID alignment and its `#error`; mt19937 UUID generator; `io_context_compat` lacking `#error`; `async_connect` constraint; `NetUtils.h` gate (`public-header-hygiene-deferral.md`, `residual-cxx-findings-deferral.md`).
- Filesystem: `copyDirectoryWithContents` special-file skip policy; `fs::copy` `ec` overload; `os::copy` shim gate (`residual-cxx-findings-deferral.md` item 2, `medium-severity-findings-f11-f17-plan.md`).
- Loader/JNI/Windows: deleted move assignment on `ManifestT`/`PlatformIdentityT`; `manifestVersion` check; JVM create-attempted latch; `JavaVirtualMachine.h` standalone-compile gap on non-Windows; `LoadLibraryExW` flags and the two recorded byte-widening sites `OSImplWindows.h:2806`, `:3396` (`windows-only-residual-findings-deferral.md` item 9); the `_InterlockedExchangeAdd` shim bound.
- Coverage gaps listed under "Coverage gaps still open" in `pr-review-fable51-deep-dive-status.md` are known and are not re-derived here.
- Provisioning scripts: out of scope by decision (`devenv7-scripts-record-not-fix`).

Two adjacent items are carried only as residuals: the Windows `std::copy` byte-widening in `createProcess` (W-2) is a third site the item-9 record did not list; the `AsyncExecutor` TSan inversion (A-12) is closed with a concrete answer rather than re-filed.

## What was verified as correct (highlights, so it is not re-reviewed)

`om::ObjPtr`/`ObjPtrCopyable`/`ProxyImpl` refcount and lock hand-off; `SharedPtrImpl`; `ExecutionQueueImpl` generation logic, NOEXCEPT bodies (intrusive lists, allocating paths in `try`); `notifyReadyImpl` lock scoping; `BL_TASKS_HANDLER_*` exception classification; `AsioSslStreamWrapper` lifetimes and shutdown chaining; `NetUtils` IPv4/ICMP header bounds; `CommandBlock` framing and `chkPartialDataTransfer`; the `TransferOnly` GET path (`SecureAlloc` wipes before exposing capacity: no stale-byte leak); `RSA_set0_*` ownership; `RsaSignVerify` (digest fixed, `RSA_verify` length-checks); `chk2InitCrypto` locking; `extractSecurityPrincipal` anchoring; client-supplied `securityPrincipal` rejected by the broker; JNI reference lifetimes and `CHECK_JAVA_EXCEPTION` coverage; `Pool.h` reuse contract; `Watchdog` atomics; `getenv` copy under lock; `os::fseek/ftell` 64-bit; `fastAbort()` async-signal-safety; cmdline duplicate/required option handling; the packager's `symlink_status`-based type detection; `renameToMakeVisibleAs` not clobbering an existing target.

# Detailed findings

Legend: **Verification** = how the finding was established (reviewer read = I re-read the cited lines; agent trace = partition reviewer's trace, quotes spot-checked). **Design intent** = what the surrounding code, comments, or prior records say the author intended; the fix must preserve it unless stated. **Perf** = runtime impact of the fix.

## A. Object model, tasks, timers, thread pool

### A-1 `TimerTaskBase::runNow()` / `cancelTask()` re-arm a live wait and create a self-sustaining zero-delay handler cascade — High — reviewer read
- `tasks/TaskBase.h:1698-1709` (`scheduleTimerInternal`), `:1728-1761` (`onTimer`), `:1788-1799` (`cancelTask`), `:1816-1824` (`runNow`).
- `scheduleTimerInternal()` calls `expires_from_now()` (which cancels the armed wait; its handler is queued with `operation_aborted`) and then `async_wait()`, so after `runNow()` two `onTimer` invocations are in flight. `onTimer` treats `operation_aborted` exactly like a fired timer, calls `run()` and re-arms with `expires_from_now( duration )`, which cancels the wait the *other* handler armed. Every handler therefore cancels the wait armed by the previous one and the timer never sleeps: `run()` executes back-to-back on a pool thread until it returns `neg_infin`. `cancelTask()` has the same structure but converges because every `run()` override checks `isCanceled()` first; `runNow()` does not converge. Production caller: `messaging/ProxyBrokerBackendProcessingFactory.h:1276` (`setClientsPruneIntervals`) → `tasks/TasksUtils.h:141` (`SimpleTimer::runNow`) → the prune callback spins. No test exercises `TimerTaskBase::runNow()` (`TestIO.h:996,2176` are `ScopeGuard::runNow`).
- Design intent: the comment at `:1793-1796` shows re-arming is the deliberate way to force an immediate `run()`; the intent (run once now, then keep the period) is kept by the fix.
- Perf: none; `cancel()` is cheaper than cancel + re-arm.
- Fix: in both places call `m_timer->cancel()` (the aborted handler already runs `run()` and re-arms), so exactly one handler stays in flight. Test: `runNow()` on a 1 s timer, assert the callback count after 100 ms is 2.

### A-2 `TimerTaskBase::wakeUp()` replaces `m_timer` with no lock — Medium — reviewer read
- `tasks/TaskBase.h:1803-1814` vs `:1700` (`onTimer` under `m_lock` uses `m_timer->expires_from_now`) and `:1816-1824` (`runNow()` correctly locks).
- `wakeUp()` calls `resetTimer()` (`SafeUniquePtr::reset` to a new `deadline_timer`) while `onTimer` may be dereferencing the old object: use-after-free of the timer; two concurrent `wakeUp()` calls race on the unique pointer.
- Design intent: the comment wants "cancel and replace with a fresh timer"; the mechanism is sound (only one handler in flight), only the locking is missing.
- Fix: `BL_MUTEX_GUARD( base_type::m_lock )` and test `base_type::m_state` directly, mirroring `runNow()`; after A-1 both can share one implementation. Perf: none.

### A-3 `RetryableWrapperTask::continuationTask()` writes `m_wrappedTask`, `m_retrying`, `m_currentRetryCount` outside `m_lock` — Medium — reviewer read
- `tasks/TaskBase.h:2051-2106`; readers `:425-437` (`getTargetTask` under `m_lock`), `:321-392` (`ForwarderTaskBase` forwards incl. `requestCancel`).

### A-4 `onTaskStoppedNothrow()` is invoked with the task lock held; `TcpServerBase` assumes the opposite and blocks in `forceFlushNoThrow( true )` — Medium — reviewer read
- `tasks/TaskBase.h:544-585` (guard opened at `:545`, hook called at `:585`, scope closes at `:709`); override `tasks/TcpBaseTasks.h:1690-1718` ("Note that the task lock isn't held while this callback is called"); observer `:1743-1767` takes `base_type::m_lock` at `:1764`.

With ≥4 live connections completing, all I/O threads park on the server lock, the remaining connections cannot run their cancel handlers, the flush never returns: global I/O-pool deadlock.
